📋 How to import your daily stock report

  1. Open your stock report Excel file (e.g. SS & CO ITEM STOCK REPORT.xls)
  2. Select the data rows (medicine name, stock quantity, price columns)
  3. Copy (Ctrl+C) and paste below — Tab-separated Excel data is automatically detected
  4. Map your columns (Name, Stock, Price) using the dropdowns
  5. Review the preview and click Import to Stock
