Pharmacy GST Compliance Tips
GST Rates for Medicines
• 0% — Life-saving medicines (WHO list, specified)
• 5% — Most medicines (HSN 3004)
• 12% — Ayurvedic/Homeopathic/Unani
• 18% — Cosmetics, Vitamin supplements (some)
Key Requirements
• GSTR-1 filed by 11th of following month
• GSTR-3B filed by 20th of following month
• Maintain purchase bills for ITC claims
• Invoice must show GSTIN, HSN, tax rate
Invoice Requirements
• Bill value > ₹200 → must issue invoice
• Include: GSTIN, Date, Customer name
• Show CGST + SGST separately
• Serial number on every invoice
Export for CA/Tally
• Use GSTR-1 CSV button → upload to GST portal
• Use GSTR-1 JSON → import to Tally ERP
• Bill-wise CSV → share with CA for reconciliation
• HSN summary → for GSTR-1 table 12
