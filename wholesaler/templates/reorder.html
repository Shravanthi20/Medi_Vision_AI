How the bot decides (click to expand)
Velocity = units sold per day, measured from your own invoiced orders over the lookback window.
Days of cover = current stock ÷ velocity — how long you'll last.
Flagged if cover < (lead time + safety buffer), or stock is at/below the item's own reorder level.
Suggested qty = enough to reach the target cover, raised to the item's MOQ, then rounded up to a scheme break-point (e.g. 10+1) when that's within reach — because a marginally bigger order that earns free goods is strictly better value.
